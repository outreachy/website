The Outreachy organizers will be tweeting from the @outreachy Twitter account. Outreachy organizers will pose the questions below. Applicants, mentors, and past interns will answer. Outreachy organizers will wait about 5-10 minutes between questions.

The questions are prefaced with Q1, Q2, etc. You will need to reply with A1, A2, at the start of you answer. Please also use the hashtag #OutreachyChat in all your tweets related to the chat.

The following questions are used for the chat:

Q1: For interested applicants: What questions do you have for mentors, coordinators, past interns, or organizers? #OutreachyChat

Q2: For past interns, mentors, and coordinators: Please introduce yourself. What community are you involved with? #OutreachyChat

Q3: For past interns, mentors, and coordinators: What do you enjoy about participating in Outreachy? #OutreachyChat

Q4: For mentors: What project are you mentoring? What skills are required to apply, and what can applicants learn? #OutreachyChat

Q5: For past interns: What community did you intern with? What was your project? What did you learn during the internship? #OutreachyChat

Q6: For past interns, mentors, and coordinators: What is the Outreachy application process like? Do you have advice for interested applicants on how to stand out? #OutreachyChat

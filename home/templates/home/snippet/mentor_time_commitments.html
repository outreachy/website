Mentor Time Commitments

Outreachy mentors are unpaid volunteers who help applicants and interns. Here’s what to expect:

  • Mentors typically volunteer 5 to 10 hours per week during the contribution period. These hours are split among all applicants to their project.
  • During the internship period, mentors are required to volunteer at least 5 hours per week. This includes time communicating with interns, reviewing contributions, and other mentoring activities.
  • Mentors are not paid staff of Outreachy or Outreachy’s fiscal sponsor (Software Freedom Conservancy).
  • Most mentors volunteer their time on evenings or weekends, though some may be paid for open source work.
  • Mentors may post their office hours in the project description, so check the project page for availability.
